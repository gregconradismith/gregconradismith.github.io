Model and parameters

Each stimulus applies a 0.5 ms inward current pulse (100 µA/cm²) at the start of the cable. The pulse covers the first 0.6 cm for an unmyelinated axon and the first node for a myelinated axon. Later pulses start from the current voltages and channel states. A pulse delivered during the refractory period can produce a smaller response or fail to launch a spike.

With sealed ends, no axial current leaves the cable. Periodic boundaries join the two ends into a ring. An ordinary stimulus on a rested ring launches two waves that extinguish when they meet. Stimulate one way applies the same pulse and briefly shunts the upstream segment toward rest to suppress the backward wave. The shunt conductance is 30 mS/cm² for 2.5 ms in the unmyelinated axon and 2 ms in the myelinated axon. After the shunt turns off, the wave can pass through the join. The lap counter tracks forward crossings at the join after the most recent one-way launch.

In myelinated mode, Hodgkin–Huxley channels regenerate the spike only at the marked nodes of Ranvier. The internodes are passive, with capacitance 0.18 µF/cm² and leak conductance 0.01 mS/cm². The plots show the passive voltage between nodes. Probes record at nodes in this mode. Nodal widths and spacings are enlarged for this teaching model.

Changing the axon type, length, diameter, or boundary condition starts a new run. The time slider reviews the most recent — of recorded voltage. Stimulate applies a pulse at the latest simulated time. First-spike measurements use crossings before the second stimulus.

The cable equation balances membrane capacitance, axial current, applied current, and the Hodgkin–Huxley sodium, potassium, and leak currents. With consistent units, the axial term is (d / 4Ri) ∂²V/∂x², where d is the diameter. Each compartment has voltage-dependent gates m, h, and n, with conductances gNa = 120m³h, gK = 36n⁴, and gL = 0.3 mS/cm². The reversal potentials are +50, −77, and −54.387 mV, respectively.

Active membrane capacitance is 1 µF/cm² and axial resistivity is 35.4 Ω·cm. Gate rates use Q10 = 3 relative to 6.3 °C, evaluated here at 18.5 °C.
