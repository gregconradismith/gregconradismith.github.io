Interactive simulations and games for exploring mathematical models and practicing qualitative reasoning. Each runs directly in your browser, with no installation required.

### Dynamical systems and excitable cells

- [Morris–Lecar simulator]({{ '/simulations/morris-lecar-simulator/' | relative_url }}): Explore membrane voltage, phase planes, excitability, oscillations, and saddle manifolds in a reduced conductance-based model.
- [Slow passage through Hopf]({{ '/simulations/slow-passage-simulation/' | relative_url }}): Compare supercritical and subcritical Hopf normal forms under parameter ramps, including delay, memory, and noise.

### Biophysics and qualitative reasoning

- [Current–voltage relation game]({{ '/simulations/current-voltage-relation-game/' | relative_url }}): Practice interpreting reversal potentials, activation, conductance, and slope from current–voltage curves.
- [Voltage-clamp game]({{ '/simulations/voltage-clamp-game/' | relative_url }}): Infer reversal potentials from voltage-clamp current traces, then reveal the underlying relationships.
- [PolyQual game]({{ '/simulations/polyqual-game/' | relative_url }}): Compare polynomial curves using the signs of the function and its first two derivatives.

### Probability and perception

- [Retaliation simulation]({{ '/simulations/retaliation-simulation/' | relative_url }}): Explore stochastic event frequency and size, then compare expected and observed activity. This stylized perception game is a toy model, not a validated model of conflict escalation.

The simulations retain their scientific references and source repository identifiers and exact source commits. Existing standalone app links remain available. [Source commits and provenance]({{ '/simulations/provenance.json' | relative_url }}).
